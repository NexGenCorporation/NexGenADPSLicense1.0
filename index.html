Part 2: Mandatory Data Protection & Privacy Addendum

5. Baseline Privacy Standard (Stricter than Swiss FADP)

Any deployment, hosting, or execution of this Work that processes personal data must enforce technical and organizational protections that exceed the baseline requirements of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). At a minimum, the following requirements apply:

  • Zero-Knowledge & End-to-End Encryption: Personal data must be encrypted at rest and in transit using strong, current cryptographic standards. Systems must be designed so operators cannot access plaintext user data without the user’s explicit authorization and control of the necessary keys.
  • Strict Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation: No personal data, telemetry, or metadata may be collected, logged, or retained beyond what is strictly necessary for the immediate execution of the specific service requested. Data may not be repurposed for unrelated activities, and must be securely deleted when no longer necessary.
  • Prohibition on Unauthorized Data Brokering: Personal data processed by this Work shall never be sold, rented, monetized, or shared with third-party data brokers or advertising networks.
